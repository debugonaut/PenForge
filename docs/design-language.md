# Penforge — accepted design language

Recorded 2026-10-19 from the user's design revisions and the accepted frontend. Use this with [document.md](../document.md), the [original quality update](specifications/design-quality-update.md), and [motion design](motion-design.md). These later decisions take precedence where the original brief proposed a different composition.

## Product and communication

Penforge turns personal handwriting into a usable font, then lets people use it in digital notes, letters and cards. The landing must make this understandable within the first viewport. Keep copy short and let visible transformations, useful previews, icons and direct manipulation explain the process.

The user's explicit 2026-10-20 direction applies across all remaining phases: the
person's goal is to get their handwriting converted into a digital font. Center the
experience on providing handwriting, reviewing the result, trying it and getting the
font. Processing should happen behind the scenes. Keep technical pipeline controls,
job mechanics, model/registry terminology, snapshot lineage and validator reports in
internal tools and engineering documentation. Optional analytics may show useful,
understandable insights about the person's writing or font without becoming a required
step. Communicate progress and problems through short, truthful outcome-oriented copy
and a clear next action. Preserve explicit consent and consequential user choices at
the relevant moment. Future backend work must support this simple experience; its
internal structure should not dictate the interface.

The approved direction combines RIFT's clear communication and grid organization with an original paper universe. Preserve a professional editorial feel, warm materials and a little character. Every large visual must explain a product action or make the current outcome tangible. Avoid filler feature claims, repeated prose, fake social proof and unrelated decoration.

Use negative space to establish hierarchy. Elevate broad areas through subtle paper grain, material changes, scale and a strong focal point; avoid filling every gap with objects. Grid and ruled paper should support the story rather than create background noise.

The user's 2026-10-20 login revision is the reference for product simplicity across
screens: one clear task, short labels, an obvious primary action and useful visual aids.
Design around what the person needs to do next. Show additional help when needed;
keep engineering explanations in documentation. Essential consent and deletion scope
belong at the relevant decision, without repeated explanatory panels. Preserve the
revised login rather than expanding it with more information.

### Account persistence direction — 2026-10-21

The owner wants the main product to become an account-backed application rather than
continue showcasing the local demo as the ordinary creation flow. Each person's
projects and meaningful edits must save durably to their own account: capture drafts,
review and editing progress, style/preview settings, generated-font references and
Writing Desk documents. Work must resume after refresh, signing back in and moving
to another device. Keep saving feedback concise and truthful; only show “Saved”
after the server acknowledges the relevant content. Local recovery protects pending
work and does not constitute account sync.

Make the real flow the primary experience when its configuration and acceptance are
ready. Keep demonstration content explicitly selected and distinguish sample fonts
from generated bytes. A missing identity/API/storage dependency must show a useful
unavailable state rather than simulated success. Preserve the current guest/account
claim policy, owner isolation, explicit glyph approval and separate training consent.
Signing in must never silently upload existing browser drafts; importing local work
requires an explicit user action. Preserve words, originals and recovery evidence
during the transition. The owner requested a note and next implementation prompt;
this records the destination, not a completed integration or public launch.

## Landing composition

The accepted route is [LandingPage.tsx](../src/components/landing/LandingPage.tsx), styled by [its CSS module](../src/components/landing/LandingPage.module.css). Preserve the centered chapter system:

1. **Hero:** oversized “Your handwriting. A font of its own.” above a large coral film stage, anchored character cutouts, concise create/how-it-works actions below. The handwriting transformation film is the focal point.
2. **How it works:** a connected, interactive Write / Refine / Type grid. Show actual input, glyph choice/review and typed output. Reuse the hero hand asset in Write. This is the detailed explanation after the hero.
3. **Writing Desk:** show the practical outcome through editable stationery with an obvious path to the full composer and real exports. This replaces the confusing “Where it goes” concept.
4. **Choose the finish:** a substantial interactive style proof with meaningful controls, visual endpoints and progressive disclosure. It should resemble the product's controls and feedback.
5. **Before you begin:** concise FAQ, honest current availability and a clear final create action.

Keep each section centered. The connected How it works grid and practical multi-pane product workspaces can use side-by-side internal organization. Do not revert the landing to alternating text-left/art-right sections. Each chapter aims to fit the viewport; content growth and accessibility take precedence over forcing a fixed height.

## Typography

The user explicitly approved the hero font and scale and requested the same treatment across the landing. Use locally served **DM Sans** for bold interface/display type and **Caveat Variable** for handwriting. Fraunces remains available in the studio/global stylesheet but is not the accepted landing headline face.

Current landing display settings:

| Context                   | Current treatment                                       |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Main display              | DM Sans 900; `clamp(66px, min(8.6vw, 14svh), 144px)`    |
| Hero line height/tracking | `0.88` / `-0.065em`                                     |
| Other chapter titles      | Shared display scale, approximately `0.9` line height   |
| Handwritten display line  | Caveat 650, approximately `1.06em`, tracking `-0.045em` |
| Hero at ≤820px            | `clamp(54px, 10vw, 82px)`                               |
| Hero at ≤620px            | `clamp(46px, 13vw, 72px)`                               |
| Hero at ≤360px            | `44px`                                                  |

Treat these as the accepted baseline and preserve their effect when fixing responsiveness. Large headlines can meet or sit visually behind the demo to create depth, while character silhouettes must clear readable letters. Keep controls, labels, error text and body copy at practical readable sizes; the display-scale rule does not apply to every UI label. Font loading must precede measurements used for handwriting or exported text.

## Characters, hand and layering

The original fountain pen and feather quill live in [Characters.tsx](../src/components/brand/Characters.tsx). They have distinct silhouettes and a restrained friendly rivalry. Preserve their authored SVG artwork and recognizable palette. Their helpful personalities should feel professional enough for the stationery product.

The user specifically rejected small, detached hero characters. Both cutouts must be **large and physically attached behind the film stage**, as though emerging from its edges. Keep them in the same responsive positioning container as the film. Current desktop character height is `clamp(320px, 40svh, 410px)` with portions hidden behind the stage; compact layouts move them above the stage with reserved clearance. The stage sits in front of their lower portions. Preserve current approved dimensions and anchors while integrating services.

The layered composition can create a wallpaper/foreground depth effect, but faces, silhouettes and movement must not cover the headline, playback controls, primary action or touch targets. On narrow/short viewports, change spacing and composition before shrinking the cutouts into detached icons.

The approved writing hand is a transparent generated [WebP](../public/illustrations/writing-hand-v2.webp), not an SVG. Its [PNG source](../public/illustrations/writing-hand-v2.png) and [generation prompt/provenance](../public/illustrations/writing-hand-v2.prompt.txt) are preserved. Use the same hand in the hero and Write demonstration. Preserve the nib anchor and measured letter advances; avoid replacing the grip or attaching a pen to an arbitrary cursor position.

Mascots react to actual hover, chapter or workflow events. In the creation workspace they become quiet guides. They do not block actions, chat at the user continuously, or modify glyph data through a theatrical gesture.

## Writing Desk and stationery

The Writing Desk is a useful creation surface. [WritingDesk](../src/components/writing/WritingDesk.tsx), [PaperThumbnail](../src/components/shared/PaperThumbnail.tsx), and [the renderer](../src/lib/writing.ts) must agree on the selected design.

The full desk reads left to right as three paper cards: the words card (format switch, title, recipient, note, signature and the device-save line), the live paper preview with its own toolbar and fit/zoom control, and an Appearance rail that leads with the fine-tune disclosure, then typeface, paper, accent and ink. Keep the preview the widest panel. The format switch keeps the three paper miniatures (Letter, Note, Card) that preview the selected stock and accent; do not replace it with an icon-only segmented control. Typeface import stays a visible action while the private export-ID field and the fine-tune controls stay disclosed. Below 1180px the Appearance rail stacks under the words card while the preview keeps the wide column; at 780px and under the order becomes preview, words, appearance. The compact landing desk keeps its existing two-column composition.

On desktop the middle card is the fixed stage: it sticks below the page top while the words and Appearance columns scroll past it, and the sheet is measured to fit that stage at its true aspect ratio rather than a fixed width. Zoom multiplies the fitted size inside the stage and stays scrollable, while Fit returns it. Short viewports and single-column layouts release the pin and let the page scroll normally. Print and exports ignore the pinned presentation.

The desk earns its extras without leaving the three-card composition: occasion templates and saved sign-offs in the words card, a browsable font library under the typeface picker with honest availability labels, a coverage strip only when the active font cannot draw characters actually on the page, editor-only baseline guides and a naturalness slider in Fine-tune, focus mode (`⌘⇧F`, Escape to leave) that keeps the middle card and hides the columns, PDF page sizes in the preview toolbar, and search, filter, sort and confirmed bulk delete in My pages. Naturalness wobbles lines deterministically and is stored in the document, so the preview and every export agree; guides never enter an export. Account and demo project rows apply their style to the sample letters and must never be labelled as a generated personal font.

| Format | Distinct structure                                                                             |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Letter | Personal stationery with monogram, header/footer rules, generous margins, greeting and signoff |
| Note   | Memo band, folded corner, subtle perforated/finished lower edge                                |
| Card   | Accent outer panel, cream inner frame, restrained corner ornaments and fold detail             |

Cotton, ruled, graph and kraft are **paper stocks independent of document format**. Format changes preserve title, greeting, body and signature. Ink and stationery accents are separate choices:

| Palette | Choices                                                             |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ink     | Navy `#192e35`, Berry `#74434b`, Forest `#315c49`, Violet `#594c87` |
| Accent  | Rose `#d7836f`, Forest `#507662`, Blue `#557e95`, plus a custom colour |

Ink and accent also accept any colour through the custom swatches; the saved document already stores any valid hex value. The desk flags an ink/paper pairing below 3:1 contrast with a short note instead of silently allowing unreadable writing, and Fine-tune ends with a reset to the default size, weight, spacing, slant, line height and alignment. New account pages keep the current palette and handwriting settings.

Use real layout differences and previews rather than changing only a label or background. Keep the paper preview prominent and readable. Personalize and More controls disclose detail without collapsing the preview or creating a large empty mat. The compact landing desk caps a letter preview at 460px; the full desk fits the whole sheet to its stage instead. Mobile uses available width with no cap. Long content grows the document within supported limits; do not clip it or shrink every word to preserve a fixed mockup.

The preview and export use the same SVG renderer and measurements, including actual imported fonts. Preserve PNG/SVG output, embedded fonts and applicable license metadata, supported device sharing, print/PDF, local drafts and full content/style handoff from the landing desk. Font export and document export are different capabilities; communicate their actual availability accurately.

Keep form controls aligned and precise even when illustrations have handmade imperfections. Use familiar icons or authored SVGs with short labels where they explain actions. Provide descriptive accessible names, visible keyboard focus, state feedback beyond color and practical touch targets. Expanded content must remain reachable without fighting section navigation.

## Freeform handwriting-to-font direction — 2026-10-24

The owner clarified the core experience: someone writes any English word or sentence
naturally on the live canvas, or supplies handwriting; analysis turns it into clean
font characters matching the supplied writing, which the person can edit individually
through the existing wizard, approve, preview and export. Keep that wizard and its
accepted layout. A compulsory isolated-letter capture workflow does not fulfil this
direction; isolated drawing remains useful for corrections or gaps.

Recognition identifies the text; character/stroke alignment and faithful extraction
must preserve the person's actual shapes. Preserve observed variants and expose
uncertainty for review. Missing characters require a clearly identified style-matched
proposal or optional additional evidence; do not represent an unseen letter as an
exact extraction. Exact fidelity across arbitrary print/cursive samples is a quality
target, not an established capability. The existing isolated-letter research and
round-brush geometry do not establish that engine. Keep unavailable capabilities
truthful, owner approval explicit, and existing research-execution restrictions intact.

## Selection editing in the Writing Desk — 2026-10-25

The owner approved selecting specific words, spaces and symbols for local document
adjustments. Keep the three-column desk and add Whole page / Selection inside
Fine-tune handwriting. Use ordinary native left-drag, keyboard selection or a native
touch selection in the words editor; the paper also supports text selection. Right-click
offers Fine-tune selection, with the same visible button beside Your words.

Selection controls set size, weight, spacing and vertical position. A spaces-only
selection adds Tighter / Wider. Preserve the highlight while adjusting controls, show
Mixed where existing values differ, and retain Undo/Redo and Reset selection. Whole
page exits the selection. These are page overrides, distinct from font/glyph edits.

Persist ranges with the words in local drafts and account documents. Reflow with real
font measurements and share rendering with SVG, PNG and print. Editor selection marks
and guides must never appear in the exported page. No new animation delay is needed.
